      What gets converted, and how

      Each file has its own output menu. PDF can become DOCX or TXT (text only, layout is not kept) or PNG/JPG images; scanned PDFs need OCR, which isn’t included.

      Images

      JPG, PNG, WEBP, GIF, BMP. Placed on a page, scaled to fit, orientation picked from the image.

      Word (DOCX)

      Headings, lists, tables, bold/italic and embedded images are kept. Complex layouts, headers and footers are simplified.

      Excel & CSV

      XLSX, XLS and CSV. Every sheet becomes a table, with values as saved in the file.

      PowerPoint (PPTX)

      Text from each slide is extracted. Images, shapes and styling are not reproduced.

      HTML & text

      HTML, TXT, MD, JSON, LOG. Scripts and external resources are removed for safety.

      Needs a server

      DOC, PPT, ODT and RTF can’t be converted accurately in a browser. Re-save them as DOCX or PPTX first, or use a server tool such as LibreOffice.

      Text-based files are drawn as page images so every language and alphabet renders correctly. The PDF looks right but its text can’t be selected.

      Privacy

      Files are read and converted by your browser. They are never sent to a server, and there is no tracking or analytics. The converter libraries and fonts load from public CDNs, which see that the page was opened but never your files. History lives in memory and disappears when you close or reload the tab.
